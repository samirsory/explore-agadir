Useful Information

Currency: Moroccan Dirham (MAD)

Languages: Arabic and Amazigh; French is also widely used.

Airport: Agadir Al Massira Airport (AGA).

Transport: Taxis, buses and arranged transfers. Confirm fares before travelling.
